# Hi, I'm Serina 👋

I'm a UX & Product Designer studying **Statistics (B.S.) with a minor in UX Design** at the University of Michigan (May 2028).

I design digital products and also build them: I take a design from research and Figma all the way to a live, responsive website. My statistics background shapes how I work with data, from survey synthesis to charts that separate real trends from noise.
